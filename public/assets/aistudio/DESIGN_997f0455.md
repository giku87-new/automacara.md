## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-precision industrial aesthetic engineered for heavy machinery, crane lifting operations, and structural civil logistics. The brand ethos prioritizes sheer structural reliability, rapid operational dispatch, and technical precision over ornamental design. 

The visual style blends modern technical industrialism with high-contrast functionalism:
- Heavyweight structural grounding: Anchored by deep technical slate surfaces that echo reinforced steel, counterweights, and heavy fabrication bays.
- High-visibility signaling: High-grade equipment yellow accents establish unmissable focal points, urgent operational dispatches, and mission-critical contact vectors.
- Architectural clarity: Razor-sharp component silhouettes, deliberate technical borders, and high-density data matrices communicate mechanical integrity and load-bearing confidence.
- Target audience response: General contractors, civil engineers, site managers, and municipal coordinators must immediately feel that this platform delivers unyielding equipment reliability, uncompromising safety compliance, and immediate field availability.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around dynamic optical contrast, emulating heavy civil equipment working under high-contrast industrial illumination.

- **Primary (`#EAB308`)**: Industrial Signal Yellow. Used exclusively for high-priority calls-to-action, urgent dispatch triggers, active machine statuses, and key operational metrics.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Deep Amber Warning. Applied to load-limit indicators, warning alerts, auxiliary action triggers, and active toggles.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`)**: Blueprint Cyan. Used sparingly for technical schematics, radius diagrams, hydraulic readouts, and non-critical dimensional metadata.
- **Neutral Surface Ecosystem**:
  - `neutral-950` (`#090D16`): The bedrock canvas, grounding all viewport layouts.
  - `neutral-900` (`#0F172A`): Structural surface level for background containers and data racks.
  - `neutral-800` (`#1E293B`): Elevated technical panel backgrounds, machine profile cards, and operational modules.
  - `neutral-700` (`#334155`): Rigid perimeter borders, load-chart dividers, and structural outlines.
- **Contrasting Insets & Cards**: Pure crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with dark slate typography are leveraged strategically for print-like technical spec sheets, invoice summaries, and high-contrast site compliance badges.
- **Functional States**: Success states rely on vibrant emerald (`#10B981`), critical warnings use intense safety vermilion (`#EF4444`), and informational items utilize tertiary blueprint cyan.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces structural discipline:

- **Display & Headlines (`Chivo`)**: Heavy industrial cuts (weights 700 to 900) evoke precision die-cast steel markings and structural blueprints. High kerning tightness on display sizes gives equipment designations and tonnage capacities extreme visual gravity.
- **Body & Data Prose (`DM Sans`)**: Provides crystalline legibility against high-density dark backgrounds. Low contrast geometric shapes ensure operational descriptions, hydraulic specifications, and safety guidelines remain readable under direct field glare.
- **Labels & Micro-Data (`Chivo`)**: Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`). This treatment mirrors safety-stencil typography on mobile telescoping cranes and heavy machinery chassis.
- **Numeric & Metric Styling**: Machine tonnage (e.g., "50 TONE", "40 METRI") must always utilize `Chivo` weight 800 or 900 to emphasize capacity ratings instantaneously.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is structured on a strict 12-column architectural grid engineered for dense equipment matrices and operational specs.

- **Grid Discipline**:
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` section margins. Technical comparison matrices and fleet catalogs span full multi-column bands.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Equipment specs adapt to a 4/4 dual-column stack.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Single-column linear flow prioritizing immediate direct dispatch, phone calling triggers, and essential crane boom/radius specs.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Component padding relies strictly on an industrial 4px/8px modular scale. Dense data clusters (load charts, technical attributes) leverage compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps, while section dividers use bold `space-2xl` rhythmic pacing to delineate machine tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established through high-contrast tonal containment and technical architectural borders rather than soft, diffuse lighting effects:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Surface)**: Deepest slate (`#090D16`). Pure structural base for full-width viewports.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panel / Bay)**: Neutral slate (`#0F172A`) framed with a 1px solid border of `#1E293B`.
- **Level 2 (Active Modules & Equipment Containers)**: Elevated technical slate (`#1E293B`) framed with `#334155`. Displays a sharp, razor-thin inner top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) simulating chamfered metallic edges.
- **Level 3 (Overlay Sheets & Urgent Dispatch Drawers)**: High-density slate (`#1E293B`) accented by a hard, directional industrial drop shadow: `0 8px 0px 0px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. No feathered blur; depth is physical and mechanical.
- **Critical Focus & Highlight States**: Interactive active elements emit an intense, tight 2px outer stroke of `#EAB308` with zero shadow blur, reinforcing the precision calibration of civil instruments.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is defined by `roundedness: 1` (0.25rem / 4px base radius).

- **Technical Chamfer Philosophy**: Heavy construction interfaces reject excessive pill shapes or roundness. Surfaces mimic sheared metal plates and milled structural components.
- **Scale Application**:
  - Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, telemetry chips): `0.25rem` (4px).
  - Cards, specification tables, and equipment profiles: `0.5rem` (8px, `rounded-lg`).
  - Hero fleet modules and modal overlays: `0.75rem` (12px, `rounded-xl`).
- **Dividers & Outlines**: Clean, continuous lines with absolute geometric precision. Diagonal hazard striping (45-degree alternate `#EAB308` / `#0F172A` hash) is reserved exclusively for system alert ribbons, heavy machinery load caps, and rapid-dispatch headers.
